I help teams build fast and reliable embedded and high-performance software,
from prototyping to production.

* Architecture, design, implementation, and review of your embedded C++ systems.
* Profiling and optimization of your high-performance applications on CPU (ARM,
  x86_64) and GPU (CUDA, OpenCL), either bare-metal or RTOS (Linux, FreeRTOS,
  QNX).
* Bring-up from scratch of Bazel projects, or migrations from legacy build
  systems, with hermetic, fast, and reproducible builds that scale to the size
  of your organization.
* Algorithm R&D in computer vision and signal processing.

[technical blog](posts).
